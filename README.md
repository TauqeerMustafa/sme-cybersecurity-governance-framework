### 01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This repository houses an institutional-grade governance suite designed to secure the digital and financial foundations of modern SMEs. In a landscape of rising ransomware and regulatory pressure, this framework provides the technical controls and policy documentation necessary to transition from reactive IT practices to a resilient, risk-informed security posture.

### 02. STRATEGIC PILLARS
| Pillar | Focus | Business Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **GOVERNANCE** | Policy Architecture | Establishes legal defensibility & compliance baseline. |
| **RISK ENGINE** | Quantitative Auditing | Maps technical threats to fiscal impact metrics. |
| **RESILIENCE** | Continuity Planning | Guarantees business uptime via immutable recovery. |
| **IDENTITY** | Zero-Trust Enforcement | Mitigates unauthorized access to financial ledgers. |

### 05. IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP
The framework is deployed in four distinct operational phases:

* **PHASE I: GOVERNANCE BASELINE**
    * Define PII handling procedures, mandate MFA, and distribute the Master Policy.
* **PHASE II: RISK QUANTIFICATION**
    * Execute the Risk Assessment Engine to map the current attack surface.
* **PHASE III: HARDENING & IAM**
    * Deploy zero-trust access protocols and automated credential rotation.
* **PHASE IV: CONTINUITY & RECOVERY**
    * Validate immutable backup integrity and conduct tabletop incident simulations.
